A human cell simulated gene by gene: what was built and what it does

Every protein-coding gene is transcribed, translated and degraded; metabolism, signalling, stress responses, the cell cycle and death are coupled through one shared state. These are the analyses run so far and the numbers they produced.

Limits

What the model does not do

  • About half of the expressed genes have no modelled function; knocking them out changes nothing.
  • Signalling and regulatory strengths are set by rule, not fitted, so most transcriptional fold changes are too small.
  • Metabolism is steady-state flux balance: no metabolite concentrations, and it misses methotrexate and glucose dependence.
  • Two thirds of half-lives are defaults; module thresholds are textbook time scales, not HeLa fits.
  • No space, organelle dynamics, explicit phosphorylation states or epigenome. Only HeLa has been run end to end.
  • HeLa-specific DepMap scores could not be downloaded, so validation uses pan-cell-line core-essential genes.
